📊 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

Key insight first: new listings peaked in 2015, dipped in 2016–17 as cities tightened short-term rental regulations (even as Airbnb turned profitable that year), recovered through 2018–19, then COVID-19 wiped out the gains in 2020. Six years of platform lifecycle in one line chart.

I built a 3-page interactive dashboard analyzing Airbnb's global performance across 10 major cities — listing trends, market concentration, guest ratings, and host trust signals.

✨ Highlights:

1️⃣ 2.79L+ listings | 1.82L+ hosts | 5.37M reviews analyzed
2️⃣ 📈 Listing growth mapped 2008–2020 across six lifecycle stages: Introduction, Growth, Maturity, Decline, Reinvention, COVID crash
3️⃣ 🌍 Paris, New York, and Sydney together account for ~48.4% of total listings and reviews — Paris leads, likely linked to hotel prices running well above Airbnb's average there
4️⃣ ⭐ City-level ratings across Accuracy, Cleanliness, Communication, Location, and Value — Mexico City and Rio scored highest overall; Cleanliness and Value were the lowest-scoring dimensions across every city, suggesting a platform-wide pattern rather than a city-specific one
5️⃣ 🗓️ Seasonal review trends: Paris and Rome peak in European summer; New York surges Nov–Dec around the holidays
6️⃣ ✅ ~2 in 3 hosts are fully identity-verified with a profile picture — a core trust signal for the platform

Beyond the visuals: cleaning and validating raw data, building the listings–reviews relationship, writing DAX for every KPI, and layering in annotations so the dashboard tells a story rather than just displaying charts.
